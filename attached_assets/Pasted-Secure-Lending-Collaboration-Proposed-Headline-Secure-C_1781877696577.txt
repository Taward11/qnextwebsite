[Section 2]
Common Lending Collaboration Challenges
[Challenge #1]
Sensitive Information Shared Through Email
Loan applications often include:
•	Financial statements 
•	Tax returns 
•	Credit reports 
•	Business plans 
•	Collateral documents 
Email was never designed for secure lending collaboration.

[Challenge #2]
Excessive Access Permissions
Many organizations grant broad folder access because it is easier to manage.
This creates unnecessary exposure.
Questions arise such as:
•	Who accessed the file? 
•	Was it downloaded? 
•	Was it forwarded? 
•	Is access still required? 

[Challenge #3]
Third-Party Collaboration Risk
External participants frequently need temporary access.
Examples include:
•	Attorneys 
•	Appraisers 
•	Auditors 
•	Brokers 
Managing external access manually can create operational and compliance risks.
[Challenge #4]
Audit and Compliance Requirements
Financial institutions must demonstrate:
•	Appropriate access controls 
•	Data governance 
•	Activity tracking 
•	Secure handling of borrower information 
Without proper visibility, audits become difficult and time-consuming.
