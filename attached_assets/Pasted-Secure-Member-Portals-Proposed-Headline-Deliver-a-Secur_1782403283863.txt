Section 1
Member Expectations Have Changed

Today's members expect digital convenience comparable to what they receive from major banks and technology providers.

Members want to:

Upload documents online
Receive statements securely
Submit loan applications
Exchange sensitive information digitally
Access documents from anywhere
Avoid branch visits whenever possible

Unfortunately, many credit unions still rely on:

Email attachments
Physical document collection
Shared links
Multiple disconnected systems

These approaches create friction for members and increase organizational risk.
