Anthony DeCristofaro, CEO of Qnext Corp. spoke to Hunter Muller at the HMG Global CISO Executive Leadership Summit on September 15, 2021. The topic was "Safeguarding Unstructured Data in a Zero Trust Environment".

**Safeguarding Unstructured Data in a Zero Trust Environment**

**Anthony DeCristofaro,** President & CEO, Qnext

Moderator: **Hunter Muller,** Founder and CEO, HMG Strategy

As CISOs and other executives continue to adjust to a changing threat landscape, one persistent threat that is becoming an increased risk to the enterprise is the growth and use of unstructured data. This includes email data, file sharing data, Microsoft Office files and other types of intellectual property that's often left unsecured.

In this Executive Interview, **Anthony DeCristofaro**, President and CEO of Qnext Corp., will share the security risks posed by unstructured data along with how the use of a Zero Trust security model can help organizations to protect their unstructured data.
